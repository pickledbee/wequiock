Wequiock Presbyterian Church is a small, friendly congregation of the Presbyterian Church (U.S.A.), gathering on Wequiock Road just northeast of Green Bay, near New Franken and Bay Settlement in Northeast Wisconsin. Whether you have been coming for years or are looking for a church home, you are welcome here.

Our Church

Our building is a well-loved part of the Wequiock landscape: a clapboard church with a shingled gable and a square bell tower, recognized by the Wisconsin Historical Society in its Architecture and History Inventory. We belong to the Presbytery of Winnebago and are committed to answering Christ's call to mission and ministry, both here at home and around the world.

Membership

Becoming a member of Wequiock Presbyterian means joining a church family that worships, serves, and grows together.

How to become a member

  1. Visit on a Sunday. Come to worship at 10:00 a.m. and get to know the congregation.
  2. Talk with the pastor. Share a bit about your faith journey and ask any questions.
  3. Meet with the Session. Members join by profession of faith, reaffirmation of faith, or transfer of membership from another church.
  4. Be welcomed. New members are received and welcomed during Sunday worship.

Since 1888

Our History

For more than 135 years, Wequiock Presbyterian Church has been a place of worship for families in the Wequiock and Bay Settlement area, looking out over the waters of Green Bay.

"The Church that Overlooks the Bay"

That is the title of a history of our congregation written by Cheryl Harvey, with the help of family and friends, around 1995. It is where our motto comes from, and a copy was given by the church to the Brown County Library so the story would be kept for future generations.

1888

The foundation is laid

In October 1888, the foundation was laid for the First Presbyterian Church of Wequiock.

1889

The church is completed

The church building was finished in 1889. It is the same white sanctuary, with its shingled gable and square bell tower, that we worship in today.

1900 to 1938

A sister church in Humboldt

Nearby, the Humboldt Presbyterian Church served as the only Protestant church in Humboldt Township.

1943

Two churches become one

After the Humboldt congregation closed, its building was moved to Wequiock and joined to the back of our church. Our history booklet gives the year as 1943, though another local history says around 1930. That 25 by 51 foot building became our Sunday School room and Fellowship Hall, so today our church is really two historic churches under one roof.

1975

Recognized by the state

The Wisconsin Historical Society surveyed the church for its Architecture and History Inventory, describing a clapboard building in the Shingle Style with a shingled gable, decorative bargeboard, and a square tower with hooded windows.

2014

"The Case of the Missing Church"

Local historian Cletus Delvaux went looking for the lost Humboldt Presbyterian Church and found it right here, attached to Wequiock. He told the story in the Brown County Historical Society's Historical Bulletin.

Today

Still overlooking the bay

Wequiock Presbyterian Church continues to worship every Sunday at 10:00 a.m. as a congregation of the Presbyterian Church (U.S.A.) in the Presbytery of Winnebago. Rev. Steve Fewell serves as pastor, and Rev. William Kamke, who led the church for many years, is Pastor Emeritus.

Sources
